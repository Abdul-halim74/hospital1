    About Nirmol Health Trust

    Serving the people of Bangladesh with affordable healthcare since June 1980.

    Nirmol Health Trust started its journey in June 1980 with a noble vision: to serve humanity. The trust set out to provide healthcare to the people of Bangladesh at affordable cost.

    A few dedicated social workers came together with a strong determination to serve the people of the country, and that ambition took shape as Nirmol Health Trust. At launch it had 13 employees, including physicians, technical and administrative staff.

    Today the trust is one of the largest health service providers in South Asia. It runs a medical college, a nursing institute, a 300-bed specialised hospital and two general hospitals of 300 and 50 beds. It is an ISO-certified organisation with one of the best-equipped laboratories in the country.

    Laboratory and imaging reports from Nirmol diagnostic centres are accepted by Singapore General Hospital, Mount Elizabeth Hospital Singapore, Bumrungrad International Hospital Thailand and other reputed hospitals in Asia.

    Mission & vision

    “To serve the humanity.”

    Our mission is to bring the latest medical science to Bangladesh at a cost ordinary families can afford, and to keep every report and treatment to an international standard.

    Quality & technology

    Our consultants hold European and North American degrees. The laboratory has a 3 Tesla MRI, a 128-slice CT scanner and other modern imaging and laboratory equipment, introduced well ahead of much of South Asia.
